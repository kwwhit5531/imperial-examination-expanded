# Expansion event catalogue

Implemented: 15 events total. The original three remain unchanged.

| ID | Role | Title | Situation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0004 | candidate | The Rain Between the Lines | Rain finds a crack above my desk. |
| 0005 | candidate | A Name I Must Not Write | Halfway through a sentence, my brush pauses over a character used in an honored ancestor's personal name. |
| 0006 | candidate | The Last Blank Space | My argument reaches its conclusion before my allotted paper does. |
| 0007 | candidate | The Borrowed Cadence | Reading my answer silently, I hear my old teacher's voice in every sentence. |
| 0008 | candidate | A Bell Beyond the Wall | A funeral bell sounds beyond the compound wall. |
| 0009 | candidate | An Answer Without Ornament | The question asks how an official should explain an order to ordinary households. |
| 0010 | assistant | Two Doors, One Queue | While helping arrange admissions, I find two streams of arrivals converging on a narrow doorway. |
| 0011 | assistant | The Measure of a Candle | The supplies laid out for the examination include candles of noticeably different thicknesses. |
| 0012 | assistant | Names That Sound Alike | Reading the admission list aloud produces two answers from opposite ends of the courtyard. |
| 0013 | assistant | The Courtyard Echo | While checking the examination rooms, I hear a whispered instruction return from the courtyard almost word for word. |
| 0014 | assistant | A Place for Every Bundle | The storage alcove is filling with candidates' bedding, spare shoes, and travel baskets. |
| 0015 | assistant | The Empty Water Jar | The jars outside the examination rooms have been filled, but nobody has planned how to replenish them once the corridors are occupied. |

Candidates: active local examination, 20% weekly pool chance. Assistants: local assembly/preparation, 35% weekly pool chance. Both retain 14-day spacing, 12-month event cooldown, and per-activity repetition guards. Assistant events do not change candidate scores. Costs and rewards are deliberately small; paid options require five gold. Capital and palace support remains out of scope.

These are implemented scripts, not runtime-validated events. Verify natural dispatch for both roles, all options, tooltips, gold affordability, interrupted attendance, cooldowns and error logs before release.
